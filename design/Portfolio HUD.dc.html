MISSION PROFILE
Mechanical engineer working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, data, biotechnology and sustainable innovation — after 18+ years leading industrial operations inside large food companies.
My question is how emerging technologies help organisations decide better inside complex systems — industry, food systems, environment, health. I work on human-centred digital transformation, combining engineering thinking, data analysis and Lean Thinking to turn technological potential into solutions that create real value for people, organisations and ecosystems.
Four principles run through the work: people first, because performance grows where well-being grows; sustainability as a strategic driver rather than compliance; continuous improvement as a mindset; technology as an enabler of human potential. I build the digital layer myself when that is faster than buying it — which is why this site carries both a factory record and a code repository.
